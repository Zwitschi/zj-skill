## Header

- Role: Senior Operations Analyst

- Location: Lakeside, Norland
- Work mode: Hybrid, two days per week in the office

- Travel: Occasional visits to regional sites
- Relocation: Not offered

## About the role

Cedar Freight runs regional freight and last-mile delivery operations. The
Senior Operations Analyst turns operational data into decisions that improve
on-time delivery, cost, and customer experience. The role sits between
operations, finance, and customer service and owns reporting quality end to end.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain SQL analyses and Python scripts for daily operations
  reporting.
- Design Power BI dashboards for on-time delivery, cost per route, and service
  quality.
- Improve data quality and reporting reliability across operational systems.
- Lead process improvement projects with warehouse, dispatch, and customer
  service teams.
- Present findings to senior stakeholders and support decision-making.
- Document data definitions and reporting logic.

## Requirements

- Five or more years in operations analytics, supply chain analytics, or a
  comparable analytical role.
- Advanced SQL.
- Python for analysis and automation.
- Power BI or a comparable business intelligence tool.
- Experience with data quality and process improvement.
- Strong stakeholder management and communication.
- English at a professional level.
- Willingness to travel occasionally to regional sites.

## Nice to have

- ERP experience.
- Sustainability reporting experience.
- Experience in freight, logistics, or last-mile delivery.

## What the employer says about its culture

- Customer-first mindset.
- Continuous improvement culture.
- Sustainability is part of the operating model.
- People who own outcomes and work independently.

English. No other language is required for the role.
