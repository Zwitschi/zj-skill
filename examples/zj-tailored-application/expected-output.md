## Cover letter

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the Senior Operations Analyst role at Cedar Freight. The
combination of customer-first delivery, continuous improvement, and a measurable
sustainability agenda is why this role stands out to me. I have spent six years
in operations and supply chain analytics, and I want to bring that analytical
discipline to a freight operation where the results are visible every day.

My path into analytics started with a degree in industrial engineering and
continued on the operational side, first in demand planning and supplier
performance at Harbor Foods, then in daily operations reporting at Northwind
Logistics. That mix matters for this role: I understand the warehouse, dispatch,
and purchasing context behind the numbers, and I can also build the queries and
reports that make the context measurable.

At Northwind Logistics I reduced late deliveries by 18% in 2023. The change came
from consistent reporting, not a single dashboard: I built the SQL and Python
reporting behind the daily view, automated weekly reports that had taken about 10
hours, and worked with coordinators until they trusted the numbers. I also
reduced reporting errors from about 12% to 3% and trained eight coordinators on
the new process. A Power BI dashboard replaced three spreadsheets and is now part
of the daily stand-up.

What draws me to Cedar Freight is the ownership culture. I like working close
enough to operations to see whether a report changes a decision, and I am
comfortable improving a process rather than only measuring it. I have no formal
sustainability-reporting experience, and I would not claim otherwise. I do have
the data quality and process discipline that a credible sustainability view
depends on, and I want to learn that side of the business properly.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how the reporting and data-quality work at
Northwind could translate to Cedar Freight's on-time delivery and cost goals.

Sincerely,
Alex Morgan
