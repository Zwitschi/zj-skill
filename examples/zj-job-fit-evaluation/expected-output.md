### Match summary

**Qualitative: strong.** Five of eight Green terms are fully evidenced and three
are partial. The core requirements are covered: SQL, Python, data quality,
process improvement, and stakeholder work. The gaps are in tool depth and domain
specifics, not in the core method.

**Basis:** every Green term has a verdict; no hard gate fails; one location rule
passes; the only partials are Power BI, supply chain analytics phrasing, and ERP.

**Uplift potential:** existing evidence can name supply chain analytics, the
Power BI dashboard, and the ERP testing more explicitly without adding a claim.
No new tool or result is needed.

### Strengths

- SQL and Python are used daily and are supported by a certificate.
- The 18% late-delivery reduction is verified and directly relevant to freight.
- Data quality work is evidenced, with an honest confidence label.
- Training eight coordinators shows stakeholder adoption work.
- The candidate already works in logistics and supply chain.

### Gaps and risks

- Power BI evidence is one dashboard, self-reported. A deep BI-tool interview
  question is likely.
- Supply chain analytics is the right domain, but the CV does not use that
  phrase.
- ERP experience is business-side testing, not configuration or ownership.
- No freight, last-mile, or sustainability-reporting experience. These are nice
  to have, not requirements.
- Travel willingness is not recorded; the posting mentions occasional regional
  travel.

### Interview likelihood

Good. The core analytical and domain requirements are met, and the verified 18%
result gives a concrete story. The main risk is tool depth in Power BI and ERP,
which a focused answer can address honestly.

### Recommendation

**Proceed with named gaps.** Apply, using the verified delivery result and the
data-quality work as the core evidence. Do not claim deep Power BI or ERP
ownership. Prepare one example of dashboard scope and one of ERP testing.

### Salary note

Not requested and not derivable from the posting. No range is produced.

### Open items

- Confirm the candidate's willingness to travel occasionally.
- Confirm the Power BI dashboard's user group and decision impact.
- Confirm whether the ERP testing included data migration or only process
  testing.
- Confirm the Spanish B1 level if a language question arises; the role does not
  require it.
