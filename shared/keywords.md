# Keyword classification

Use this system when evaluating a posting or tailoring documents. Classify terms
from the posting, never from the candidate's CV.

## Three classes

| Class  | What belongs here                                                                                                                            | Where it may appear                                                    |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Green  | Specific technical terms, named methods, systems, certifications, and industry vocabulary that appear exactly or near-exactly in the posting | CV body, contribution, profile, and keyword table, wherever verifiable |
| Red    | General working style, communication, teamwork, independence, and cross-role soft skills                                                     | Supporting use in the CV and cover letter                              |
| Yellow | Company values, leadership culture, work environment, vision, and motivation-level language                                                  | Cover letter only                                                      |

Green is the priority. Red supports and never displaces Green. Yellow must not
appear in the contribution or profile sections.

## Verifiability

For every Green term, record one verdict:

- yes: evidenced in the candidate's documents;
- partially: adjacent evidence exists, but the exact term is not demonstrated;
- no: no evidence.

A Green term with "no" is a gap. Name it explicitly. Do not add the term to the
CV to close the gap.

## Tables

Produce three artefacts when the skill asks for a keyword analysis:

1. **Keyword list** ordered by class, with the verifiability verdict for every
   Green term.
2. **Skills match table** mapping each requirement to the candidate's evidence,
   with a short note and the verdict.
3. **Overall match summary** with a qualitative or numeric match statement as
   configured by the user, and an uplift estimate showing how much ATS coverage
   could improve by rewording existing content only.

Do not invent weights, bands, or scores that the user has not supplied. If no
scoring scheme is configured, use a qualitative summary and explain the basis.

## Placement rules

- Use Green terms only where the evidence supports them.
- Prefer the posting's exact term when it is accurate; do not force synonyms.
- Do not stuff keywords or repeat them unnaturally.
- Keep Yellow terms for the cover letter's motivation narrative.
- Do not add a keyword that changes the meaning of a role or overstates
  seniority.

## Missing terms

For every missing Green term, state:

- the term;
- why it matters in the posting;
- whether the candidate has adjacent evidence;
- whether it is learnable, a hard requirement, or a reason not to apply.
