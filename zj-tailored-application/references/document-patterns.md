# Document patterns

Neutral structural patterns for tailored documents. They show shape, not
content. Replace every placeholder with approved evidence.

## Contribution paragraph

```text
With <X years> of experience in <field>, I <role identity>. I <what I do> for
<type of organisation or user>, using <methods, tools, and ways of working>.
Across <role or project context>, I <result 1 with approved number> and
<result 2 with approved number>. I combine <strength A> with <strength B> so
that <value the employer gets>.
```

Rules:

- one paragraph, first person, about six lines;
- two or three approved results;
- no education list;
- no repetition of the profile;
- no adjective stacking.

## Profile bullets

Patterns:

```text
<Credential or degree> with <X years> in <role identity>, focused on <domain>.
<Role identity> with hands-on <Green technology> and <Green method> in
<regulated or complex context>.
<Role identity> who bridges <business area> and <technical area> across
<scale or scope>.
```

Rules:

- three to six bullets;
- one positioning idea per bullet;
- Green terms only where evidenced;
- no results or value claims;
- no Yellow terms.

## Experience bullet

Pattern:

```text
<Strong verb> <what was done> using <method or tool>, <result with approved
number or verified qualitative outcome>.
```

Examples of shape, not content:

```text
Redesigned <process> with <method>, reducing <metric> from <before> to <after>.
Led <team or workstream> across <scope>, delivering <outcome> within <timeframe>.
Introduced <practice> for <area>, improving <metric> by <approved figure>.
```

Rules:

- one idea per bullet;
- result first where possible;
- no invented figures;
- no title inflation;
- no subheadings inside a role.

## Cover letter

```text
<Opening: role, employer, and the specific reason for applying.>

<Career thread: the experience that leads to this role and why it is coherent.>

<Evidence point 1: a concrete result or capability from the approved pack.>

<Evidence point 2: a second, different kind of evidence.>

<Motivation: what the candidate wants to contribute and why this environment.>

<Close: direct next step, no flattery.>
```

Rules:

- Yellow terms carry the narrative;
- Green and Red terms support;
- no generic praise;
- no clichés;
- no repetition of the CV;
- no employer claim that is not evidenced.

## Change log

```markdown
| Section   | Before     | After      | Reason                                               | Evidence     | Confirmed |
| --------- | ---------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | --------- |
| <section> | <old text> | <new text> | keyword / impact / clarity / trim / role integration | <source tag> | yes / no  |
```

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- "passionate", "perfect fit", "dream role", "unique opportunity";
- opening with a summary of the CV;
- repeating the job ad back to the employer;
- claiming a connection to the employer that is not evidenced;
- turning every bullet into a superlative;
- adding tools or metrics that are not in the evidence pack;
- changing job titles to sound closer to the target role;
- using Yellow language in the CV;
- leaving a rewritten bullet without a change-log entry.
