# Tailoring workflow

Detailed steps for the `zj-tailored-application` skill. Read the shared evidence,
interaction, keyword, gate, output, and style rules first. This file covers the
tailoring-specific work only.

## Step 0: Preflight

Confirm:

- the baseline CV is available;
- the evidence pack is for this posting;
- the keyword map is approved;
- the posting text is available;
- output language and cover-letter language are set;
- the template and spelling convention are known;
- a confirmed trim list exists, or line-level trimming was not requested;
- role-level selection decisions are logged and confirmed.

If any required item is missing, stop. If the evaluation is stale or for another
posting, ask for a new evaluation before writing.

## Step 1: Select material

- Work from the baseline, not from an earlier tailored CV.
- Keep all sections that the posting needs.
- Choosing which roles and sections to include is role-level selection. It is
  part of tailoring, but log every decision and confirm it with the candidate.
- Condensing or omitting an entire role is role-level selection, not a line
  trim. Do not omit a role silently.
- Apply only the confirmed trim list to individual lines inside the included
  roles.
- Do not remove a line that carries a unique differentiator, continuity signal,
  or context the candidate wants to keep.
- Do not add a section that the baseline does not support.

## Step 2: Contribution paragraph

Write one paragraph.

Structure:

1. Open with a variation of "with X years of experience in ...".
2. State who I am.
3. State what I do.
4. State how I do it.
5. State what results I achieve.
6. Close with the value an employer gets.

Rules:

- first person, prose, no bullets;
- about six lines;
- use two or three of the strongest approved results;
- no education list;
- no repetition of the profile;
- no claim that is not in the evidence pack.

## Step 3: Profile bullets

Write three to six bullets.

Each bullet is one compressed positioning statement that may combine:

- education or certification;
- role identity;
- years of experience;
- named Green technologies;
- domain focus.

Rules:

- Green terms first;
- Red terms only where they add real signal;
- no Yellow terms;
- no results or value claims;
- no seniority inflation;
- no tool that the evidence pack does not support.

## Step 4: Experience integration

For each role:

1. Keep the official title exactly as it appears in the baseline.
2. Check whether a genuine overlap exists between the role and the target role.
3. If yes, adapt at most one bullet.
4. Name the target-role term explicitly inside that bullet.
5. Apply it to a real, verifiable task.
6. Quantify only with approved figures.
7. If no genuine overlap exists, leave the role unchanged.

Do not:

- change dates;
- merge roles;
- add responsibilities;
- upgrade the implied seniority;
- rewrite several bullets in one role;
- force a keyword into a role where it does not belong.

## Step 5: Impact enhancement

Use this only for bullets the evidence pack flags as task-only or partially
outcome-oriented.

For each flagged bullet:

1. Show the original bullet.
2. Diagnose it: task-only, partial outcome, or strong outcome.
3. If the outcome is already supported by the evidence pack, offer a rewrite.
4. If not, ask one short question to surface the outcome.
5. Ask the candidate to confirm, adjust, or skip.
6. Leave strong bullets untouched.

Rules:

- one bullet at a time;
- state the total number of flagged bullets before starting;
- never invent a number or outcome;
- never convert a task into a claim the candidate did not make;
- keep the candidate's wording where it is already accurate.

## Step 6: Cover letter

Structure:

1. Opening that names the role and the specific reason for applying.
2. The career thread that leads to this role.
3. Two or three concrete evidence points that support the fit.
4. The motivation and what the candidate wants to contribute.
5. A close that is direct, not flattering.

Rules:

- Yellow terms carry the narrative;
- Green and Red terms are supporting, not the focus;
- no filler opening;
- no generic praise of the employer;
- no clichés;
- no repetition of the CV;
- no claim about the employer that is not evidenced;
- do not mention salary unless the evaluation produced a salary note and the
  candidate asked for it in the letter.

## Step 7: Consistency check

Check every document against the evidence pack:

- every claim has a source;
- every number matches the approved figure and confidence;
- official titles are unchanged;
- at most one adapted bullet per role;
- Green terms used only where verifiable;
- Yellow terms only in the cover letter;
- no invented employer connection;
- no unexplained gap;
- the confirmed trims are the only removals.

If a check fails, fix it before presenting the document. If the fix needs new
evidence, stop and ask.

## Step 8: Change log and open items

For each edit, record:

- section and line or bullet;
- before and after;
- reason: keyword match, impact enhancement, clarity, role selection, trim, or
  target-role integration;
- evidence or source tag;
- confirmation status.

List open items separately:

- facts still to confirm;
- missing dates or links;
- decisions the candidate has not made;
- anything that would change the application.

## Output order

1. Tailored CV.
2. Cover letter.
3. Change log.
4. Open items.

The change log is mandatory when a baseline fact or bullet was rewritten.

## Handoff

Consume the evidence pack and keyword map from the evaluation. Do not create a
second evidence set. If a new claim is needed:

1. Stop.
2. Ask the candidate.
3. Record the answer with a confidence tag.
4. Update the pack before using the claim.

The evaluation's trim list is the only approved source of line-level removals.
Role-level selection is allowed, but it is not a trim: log it and confirm it with
the candidate. The converter's `[QUANTIFIED]` markers are authoritative; do not
ask again for those figures.

## Quality checklist

- Preconditions met.
- Baseline used, not a previous tailored CV.
- Contribution follows the six-line structure.
- Profile has three to six bullets and no results.
- Official titles unchanged.
- At most one adapted bullet per role.
- Impact enhancement confirmed bullet by bullet.
- Cover letter is Yellow-led and evidence-based.
- Every claim traces to the pack.
- Change log complete.
- Open items listed.
- Documents labelled as drafts.
